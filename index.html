Little Worlds

Your home planet and everything you build on it are saved in this browser. Scan plants, creatures and planets to fill your ship with data, then bring it home (or beam it from a satellite dish) to fill your data centers. Storing more data upgrades your warp fuel and cargo, so you can warp further out, where worlds get stranger.

Touch

Left thumb
Drag to walk, or to steer the ship
Right thumb
Drag to look around
In the ship
Hold Go to fly forward, ▲ ▼ to rise and sink. Sink onto the ground to land.
Planet names
Tap one to target it, then Face target
Hide
The Hide button next to the planet name clears the screen. While hidden, double-tap and hold on the right side to relieve yourself; let go to stop.
Build
Aim with the reticle, ⟲ ⟳ to rotate, Place. Swipe the item row for more props. Camps away from home vanish when you leave.
Warp
From the pilot seat. Warp home is always free.

Keyboard and mouse

Click
Capture the mouse to look (Esc releases it)
WASD · Space · Shift
Walk, jump, run
Ship
W go, S back, mouse or A / D to steer, Space rise, Shift sink. Sink onto the ground to land.
E · F · R
Interact, scan, recall to ship
T · V · L
Face target, change view, flashlight
Left Ctrl or C
Hold to crouch (on foot and inside the ship)
U
Hide the interface. While hidden, hold the mouse button to relieve yourself.
Planet names
Click one to target it
B
Build: click places, Q / E or wheel rotates, 1–9 and 0 pick, X remove. Scroll the build bar for more props.
N · M
Warp further out, warp home (in the pilot seat)

Settings

Erasing removes your home planet, everything you built, your stored data and cargo, and starts a brand new home.
